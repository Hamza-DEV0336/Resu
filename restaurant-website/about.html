Since 1985

A Legacy of Flavor

Guru Palace began as a small family-owned restaurant with a simple mission: to share the authentic flavors of India with our community. Over three decades later, we've grown into one of the most beloved dining destinations in the city, yet our commitment to quality and tradition remains unchanged.

Our founder, Guru Singh, brought with him recipes passed down through generations, along with a passion for hospitality that defines our restaurant to this day. Every dish we serve tells a story of heritage, craftsmanship, and love.
